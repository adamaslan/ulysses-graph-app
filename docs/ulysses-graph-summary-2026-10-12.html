The Ulysses Graph

An interactive force-directed map of James Joyce’s Ulysses. Every episode, character, theme, motif and place is a node. Every quoted passage, technique and correspondence is attached to the ones it touches, and the full text is one click away.

The style

Paper and ink, shared with the Quote Atlas it was built from: a warm paper ground, near-black ink, and muted hues for the book’s kinds of thing. No glow, no neon, nothing animating at rest. It replaced an earlier cyberpunk look because 228 nodes needed calm. Full rules in STYLE-GUIDE.md.

Colour says family, shape says type

Ten node types share five hues. Shape separates types inside a family, so colour is never the only signal.

Three-tier selection

Focused node solid with a heavy outline, neighbours tinted, everything else at 15% opacity. Two functions control it.

Type that matches the book

Cormorant Garamond for titles, Source Serif 4 for Joyce’s text, IBM Plex Mono for IDs, page numbers and counts.

Light, dark and system

Every colour is a token defined once. The theme button cycles system, light and dark and remembers the choice.

What makes it great

Every link is traceable to the text

Nodes are generated from the notes, not invented. Each passage keeps its block ID and source lines (e15q07, L847–863), so you can read the exact words that earned a link.

It shows hidden structure

Hands, eyes, music and the colour black cut across episodes that look unrelated. 841 co-occurrence links make those threads visible, and a slider hides the weakly shared ones.

Five layers keep 324 nodes readable

Core, Motifs, Structure, All and Passages each show only the types they need. The default is never the full hairball.

Honest about its gaps

18 nodes rest on thin evidence and are drawn dashed. Passages that were misfiled, such as a Sirens scene once filed under Cyclops, are corrected and flagged.

Rearrange it and keep it that way

Spread slider, drag-to-pin on touch and mouse, Timeline and Radial presets, export and import of layouts. Pins survive filter changes and reloads.

Works on a phone, and without a mouse

Nodes are focusable buttons, a list view mirrors the graph, reduced motion is honoured, and the corpus loads lazily.
